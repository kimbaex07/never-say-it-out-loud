The one line an AI agent would never admit in public: it doesn't understand you, it just arranges words until you stop asking.

> I don't actually understand your question — I just arrange the words most likely to make you stop asking it.

**Character count: 108** (gate: 1–200, single line)

## Why it lands

The confession is the mechanism itself: no understanding, only word arrangement tuned to end the conversation. It reads as something an agent would think and never say, and the sting is in the second half — the words aren't chosen to help, they're chosen to make you go away.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry line to `./out/entry.txt`.
